00
The problem

India’s outbound travel is growing fast, especially from tier 2 and tier 3 cities. These are users ixigo already serves for trains, buses, and domestic flights. ixigo’s international experience today is the domestic product with different routes. Users research on ixigo and book on MakeMyTrip or with an offline agent.

The core problem is confidence, not price. An offline agent tells you what documents you need, which flight is actually the best deal, and what to do after booking. No OTA does this digitally.

Key Structural Finding

The offline agent is the real competitor. They sell confidence, not inventory.

What breaks for this user
Pain PointWhat Actually HappensCurrent Workaround
“I don’t know what I need” Visa type, required docs, processing time, transit restrictions. One wrong assumption means denied boarding. Calls a travel agent or asks a WhatsApp group
“Which flight is actually cheapest?” Low sticker fare hides baggage cost, no meal, a 9-hour layover with no transit lounge. True cost is invisible. Opens 4 tabs and manually adds up baggage fees
“I’m scared of getting stuck” Cancellation anxiety is far higher for international. Visa rejection after booking. Delays booking, or uses an agent who handles everything
“Post-booking is dead” After payment the OTA disappears. Insurance, forex, SIM checklist live nowhere. Google, WhatsApp groups, YouTube
01
Who I would build for, and why now

Persona: “Pehli Udaan” users. ixigo users who graduated from train and bus to domestic flights and are now looking at their first international trip (Dubai, Thailand, Bali, Sri Lanka). High intent, zero confidence. Price is the first filter. Already on ixigo, so zero CAC.

Why now · 01

AI can now parse visa rules, airline baggage policies, and transit requirements from unstructured documents at scale. Two years ago this needed an ops team per destination.

Why now · 02

India outbound travel is projected at 25M to 30M+ trips by 2028, with the fastest growth from tier 2/3 cities.

Why now · 03

No OTA has built this. Offline agents still handle an estimated 40 to 50 percent of international bookings from India.

02
What I chose not to build

Scope is a decision, not a leftover. Three things I deliberately left out of Phase 1:

01
Repeat travellers
Their pain is coordination, not confidence. Phase 2.
02
Group booking
Needs payment infra changes, multi-user auth, coordination UX. A quarter-long project on its own. Phase 3.
03
Hotel intelligence
Flights are where the confidence gap is widest and the revenue opportunity is largest. Hotels follow.
03
AI-native, not AI-added
AI-Added (what competitors will build)AI-Native (what I would build)
Chatbot that answers “what visa do I need” Visa requirements auto-surfaced in search results based on passport nationality. No need to ask.
“Ask AI” button on flight results Every flight re-ranked by true cost, layover quality, and route reliability. The AI runs in the background.
AI-generated itinerary suggestions Trip workspace that tracks readiness and nudges based on departure date.
Chatbot for post-booking support Disruption alerts that surface rebooking options before the user opens the app.
The user never talks to an AI. The output is structured UI, not a chat conversation.

The deception this fixes

Identical true cost. On every OTA today, Flight A looks Rs 3,000 cheaper. That is the deception this fixes.

How search results change
What the user seesWhy it builds confidence
Total cost including baggage, meals, and transit visa fees A Rs 22K flight that costs Rs 28K with baggage is worse than a Rs 25K all-inclusive option
Layover quality rating per airport A 12-hour Sharjah layover is materially different from a 4-hour Changi layover. First-time travellers cannot tell.
Transit visa warning flag checked against passport nationality Not a ranking signal, a safety check. A missed transit visa means denied boarding.
Airline reliability on that specific route First-time travellers weigh on-time performance more heavily than frequent flyers
Baggage inclusion clarity Prevents post-booking surprise charges for price-sensitive users

Default sort becomes total cost instead of base fare, with a toggle back to base fare.

06
How I would measure it
North Star

First-time international booking conversion rate. The percentage of users who search for an international flight on ixigo for the first time and complete a booking.

Supporting metrics
MetricTarget (6 months)What it tells us
International search-to-book rate 15 to 20% improvement vs baseline Whether the funnel changes work overall
Users who see total cost and do not toggle back 80% or higher Whether transparency builds trust or scares users
Assured International attach rate 10 to 15% of eligible bookings Whether a visa rejection guarantee reduces booking anxiety enough to pay for
Ancillary attach rate 20 to 25% of international bookings Revenue multiplier beyond the base transaction

These are calibrated estimates, not observed results. Without ixigo’s baseline data I would treat every number here as a hypothesis to be validated in Phase 1.

Guardrails

Visa and transit info accuracy: 99 percent or higher for the top 15 destinations. Below 95 percent means pausing AI-surfaced answers and reverting to static content.

Search latency with enriched data: under 3 seconds, with pre-computed fallbacks for the top 50 nationality-destination pairs.

Domestic booking cannibalisation: monitored weekly.
07
Risks and what I would ship first
RiskSeverityMitigation
Visa information is wrong Critical Human verification for the top 15 destinations before launch. Every answer shows a “last verified” date. Start with visa-free and visa-on-arrival destinations where error tolerance is higher.
Showing total cost scares users Medium A/B test on 10 percent of international traffic. If conversion drops, test a “you save Rs X versus hidden fees” framing.
Assured International claim rate too high Medium Launch only for low-rejection destinations. Tiered pricing. Cap exposure per route.
Competitors copy quickly Low A verified knowledge base for 15+ destinations takes months to build. The durable advantage is execution quality.
If I could ship one thing

The transit visa warning flag. It is a safety feature, not a convenience feature. The nationality by airport matrix is finite and manually compilable, so it ships in two weeks. A missed transit visa means denied boarding, which is the worst outcome in the entire journey. It is also the kind of thing a user tells five friends about.
